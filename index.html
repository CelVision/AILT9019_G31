Core Motivation

The Social Anxiety Bottleneck on Campus

Why traditional dating and social apps trigger conversational fatigue among university students, and how activity-centered gameplay breaks the deadlock.

❌ Mainstream Social Platforms
  • Appearance-First Swiping: Drives superficial judgment and high-stakes biographical self-presentation.
  • Unguided Text Chat: Imposes the burden of crafting witty introductory messages, leading to conversational paralysis.
  • Awkward Pauses: Stagnant chat threads trigger insecurity and ghosting.
  • Harassment Exposure: Unrestricted text channels create serious safety and moderation liabilities.
✅ ConnectFour Connect Approach
  • Activity-First Gameplay: Attention is centered on a familiar 7×6 board game; turns provide organic conversational pacing.
  • Mode-Partitioned Chat: Human PvP uses 6 sportsmanlike preset phrases, 4 reaction stickers, and 4 Daily Icebreaker cards.
  • AI Companion in PvE: Alibaba Cloud Bailian's qwen3.6-flash acts as a collegiate coach for advice and banter.
  • Zero Wait-Time Availability: 15-second queue timeout guarantees instantaneous transition to an AI match if no peer arrives.
System Design

Decoupled Asynchronous Architecture

Built with Python 3.12 (FastAPI), real-time WebSockets, HTML5 Canvas 2D, and an autonomous dual-tier AI fallback subsystem.

Engineering Highlights

Key Technical Implementations

Engineered for reliability, real-time responsiveness, and complete user privacy.

🛡️

Age-Gated Matchmaking

Single-pass atomic queue tick evaluates compatible peers within |ΔAge| ≤ 3 years (Age ≥ 18). Authoritative 1 Hz heartbeat drives countdowns without relying on client timers.

⚡

Dual-Tier AI Fallback

Queries Alibaba Cloud Bailian qwen3.6-flash within a strict 3.5s deadline. Upon timeout, HTTP 429, or illegal column output, falls back to local Depth-4 Minimax in ~23.6ms.

💬

Mode-Partitioned Chat

PvP structurally omits free-form text, using 6 canned phrases and 4 Daily Icebreaker cards. PvE enables safe conversational banter with the AI Coach via a 6-turn sliding window.

🎨

Canvas 2D & Web Audio

Unified Pointer Events input layer handles multi-touch, stylus, and mouse with responsive canvas scaling. Real-time procedural audio synthesis without external MP3 dependencies.

🔒

Zero Persistent PII

Collects zero names, emails, phone numbers, or passwords. Guest nicknames and birth years exist solely in RAM for the active socket session and are wiped upon disconnection.

🐳

Hardened DevOps Pipeline

Docker container with non-root execution and read-only filesystem. Automated 8-point deployment invariant verification script (check_public_url.py) validates endpoints.

Empirical Validation

15-User Cohort Usability Evaluation

Tested with 15 university students (ages 18–24, mean 21.2) across two full sessions (live peer PvP and AI Coach PvE).

14 of 15 participants completed both matches (exceeding ≥85% benchmark).

Evaluated across UI intuitiveness, turn latency, chat comfort, icebreaker utility, and satisfaction.
